Detailed Description

Simulating one or a chain of tasks in a LIDAR scan. Simulates a task in the gaming engine box2d till it terminates. Then returns the termination message. It's not suggesting the next task but just reports why it has terminated. The configurator flavour needs to then decide what to do next.
